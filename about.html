About FindME

Making lost belongings easier to find inside our campus.

What is FindME?

FindME is a school campus lost and found website designed to help students, teachers, and school personnel recover their missing belongings.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide an easy way for the school community to report, search, and return lost belongings.

Our Goal

We aim to make the school environment more organized and helpful by connecting people with their lost belongings.
