FindME – School Campus Lost and Found Website

FindME is a school campus Lost and Found website designed to help students, teachers, and school personnel report, search for, and recover lost belongings within the school premises. Its main purpose is to make finding missing items easier and more organized by providing a place where lost and found items can be viewed. So instead of posting an lost item on social media, The website FindME will make this easier to browse. The target audience of this website is within the school community itself, especially students who may accidentally lose personal belongings such as IDs, wallets, bags, keys, and other school materials. This will also help our security personel on school campus to be able reduce their work especially on emergencies. The theme of this webiste will most likely more on slight plain background colors to be able to visualize what is being found on the webiste. Overall, Through FindME, the school community can help one another return lost items to their rightful owners.
